📜 November Challenge Rules

1. Daily 30-Minute Streak Minimum:
Every calendar day requires at least 30 minutes of logged exercise to maintain your streak. Any day under 30 minutes in the past incurs a +30 min penalty debt. Today and future days are never treated as misses!
2. 120-Minute Daily Cap:
A maximum of 120 minutes counts toward the challenge per day to encourage consistency over cramming.
3. Habit Breaker (+30 min penalty per slip):
Choose one personal habit to break. Any day you slip adds a +30 min penalty debt to your monthly target. Habit tracking can be toggled off if you prefer fitness-only.
4. 🕊️ 7-Day Habit Grace Redemption:
Maintaining a consecutive 7-day clean habit streak unlocks a Grace Token that cancels out one 30-minute penalty from your monthly total!
5. Baseline Target & 7-Day Window:
Standard baseline is strictly 900 minutes. During November 1–7, athletes who achieved ≥900 min in September can scale their baseline to their September actual volume.
