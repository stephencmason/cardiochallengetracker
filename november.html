📜 November Challenge Rules

Goal: Complete your personalized baseline volume of exercise (averaging $\ge 30$ min/day).

Baseline Target Window: Standard goal is 900 minutes. Returning finishers from September who achieved $\ge 900$ min may set their baseline to their September actual volume during the Nov 1–7 window.

Daily Exercise Cap: Up to 90 minutes counted per day towards your challenge volume.

Missed Day Penalties: Calendar days that have concluded with less than 30 minutes logged add a +30 minute penalty.

Habit Breaker & Penalty: Each habit slip recorded adds a +30 minute penalty.

🕊️ 7-Day Habit Grace Redemption: Maintaining a consecutive 7-day clean habit streak unlocks a Grace Token that automatically forgives one 30-minute penalty from your monthly total!
