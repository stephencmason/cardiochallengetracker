November Challenge Rules

🎯 Baseline Target (+5% or 900m)

If you participated in September, your base target matches your September total. New participants begin at the 900-minute baseline (30m × 30 days).

🔥 30-Minute Daily Minimum

Workouts must be at least 30 minutes to maintain your daily streak. Days with <30m count as a missed day (+30 min penalty).

🚫 120-Minute Daily Counting Cap

A maximum of 120 minutes counts toward the challenge per day to encourage steady, daily consistency and prevent unhealthy cramming.

⚡ Personal Habit Breaker

Define a personal habit to eliminate or master. Check off whether you were Clean or Slipped each day. Each slip adds +30 minutes to your monthly total.

🕊️ 7-Day Habit Grace Redemption

Maintaining a consecutive 7-day clean habit streak unlocks a Grace Token that automatically forgives one 30-minute penalty!

🏅 Achievement Badges

Unlock trophies including Century Club, Perfect 10, Perfect Game, Iron Will, Zen Master, and The Unbroken!
