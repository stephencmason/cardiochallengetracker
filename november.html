November Challenge Rules

🎯 Baseline Target (+5% or 900m)

If you participated in September, your base target matches your September total. New participants begin at the 900-minute baseline (30m × 30 days).

🔥 30-Minute Daily Minimum

Workouts must be at least 30 minutes to maintain your daily streak. Days with <30m count as a missed day (+30 min penalty).

🚫 120-Minute Daily Counting Cap

A maximum of 120 minutes counts toward the challenge per day to encourage steady, daily consistency and prevent unhealthy cramming. Any additional minutes are still recorded as actual minutes in your history.

⚡ Personal Habit Breaker

Define a personal habit to eliminate or master. Check off whether you were Clean or Slipped each day. Each slip adds +30 minutes to your monthly total.

🕊️ 7-Day Habit Grace Redemption

Maintaining a consecutive 7-day clean habit streak unlocks a Grace Token that automatically forgives one 30-minute penalty (e.g. 2 habit slips [+60m] minus 1 Grace Token [-30m] = net +30m penalty debt)!

🏅 Achievement Badges

Unlock trophies including Century Club, Perfect 10, Perfect Game, and new habit milestones: Iron Will (7-day clean habit), Zen Master (14-day clean habit), and The Unbroken (30/30 workouts and clean habits)!

📈 Leaderboard Fairness

Rankings are based strictly on % Complete ($\text{Logged} \div \text{Target including penalties}$), ensuring fair competition regardless of base target size.
