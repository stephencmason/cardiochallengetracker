📜 November Challenge Rules

1. Daily 30-Minute Streak Minimum:
Every calendar day requires at least 30 minutes of logged exercise to maintain your streak. Any day under 30 minutes incurs a +30m penalty debt.
2. 120-Minute Daily Cap:
A maximum of 120 minutes counts toward the challenge per day to encourage consistency over cramming.
3. Habit Breaker (+30m penalty per slip):
Choose one personal habit to break. Any day you slip adds a +30m penalty debt to your monthly target. Habit tracking can be toggled off if you prefer fitness-only.
4. 🕊️ 7-Day Habit Grace Redemption:
Maintaining a consecutive 7-day clean habit streak unlocks a Grace Token that cancels out one 30-minute penalty from your monthly total!
5. Baseline Target & 7-Day Window:
Returning participants carry their September target volume. During November 1–7, you can switch between your calculated September baseline and the default 900m target.
