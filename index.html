Challenge Rules & Guidelines

The goal of the September Cardio Challenge is building sustained daily discipline and consistency.

👟 Your Cardio, Your Baseline

Cardio is relative to your personal starting point. For some, it might be a dedicated power walk or hiking; for others, an indoor trainer session, gym circuit, gravel ride, or run. Any sustained, intentional effort that gets your heart rate up counts.

⏱️ The Daily Baseline

The core objective is 30 minutes of intentional cardio per day throughout September (30 days × 30 min = 900 minutes base target).

🚫 The Daily Counting Cap

A maximum of 120 minutes can count toward your challenge total on any single calendar day. You can exercise longer, but excess minutes won't bank ahead beyond the 120-minute cap.

🍺 Drink Penalty

Every alcoholic drink consumed adds a +30 minute penalty to your overall September target. Drinks directly raise the total minutes needed to finish.

❌ Missed Day Penalty

If you fail to get your workout in or mark a day as missed, a +30 minute penalty is added to your target, and you'll need to make up the volume on other days.

🎯 Adjusted Target Formula

Total Target = 900 min + (Total Drinks × 30 min) + (Missed Days × 30 min). Standings are ranked by percentage progress against each participant's adjusted target.

☁️ Cloud Sync

Sign in with your Google account in the top bar to save your entries to the live cloud database and have your progress automatically displayed on the group leaderboard.
