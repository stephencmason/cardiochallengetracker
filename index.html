Challenge Rules & Guidelines

The goal of the September Fitness Challenge is building sustained daily discipline and consistency.

👟 Multi-Activity Tracking

You can log multiple activity sessions per day (Bootcamp, Cardio, Cross Fit, Weights, Resistance Training, Yoga, Other) and add optional workout notes. Minutes from all daily sessions are totaled together.

⏱️ Customizable Daily Baseline

The standard baseline is 30 minutes per day (30 days × 30 min = 900 min base). You can increase your daily target (e.g., 45m or 60m) in the top toolbar to match your personal goal.

📈 Daily Leaderboard Movement

Keep an eye on the +/- column between your rank and name on the group standings table. It compares your standing today against where you ranked yesterday, showing how your recent sessions moved you up or down.

🔥 Daily Workout Streaks

Maintain an active daily streak by meeting or exceeding your daily minute goal consecutively. Your streak is shown on your progress card and directly next to your progress on the group leaderboard.

🏅 Secret Achievement Badges

Milestone badges are awarded automatically as you conquer unexpected achievements throughout September. Keep training, exploring new workouts, and enjoying the journey to unlock them all!

🚫 The Daily Counting Cap

A daily cap (default 120 minutes or higher if your goal exceeds it) limits how much can bank toward the challenge on a single day to encourage steady daily habits.

🍺 Optional Drink Penalty or Door Dash for Jon

When enabled, each alcoholic drink adds a +30 minute penalty to your overall September target. You can toggle this setting in the top toolbar.

❌ Automatic Missed-Day Policy

Any past September day with 0 logged minutes automatically counts as a missed day (+your daily target penalty). You can go back and enter workouts for any past day anytime to immediately erase the penalty!

🎯 Adjusted Target Formula

Total Target = (30 × Daily Goal) + (Drink Penalty if enabled) + (Missed Days × Daily Goal). Standings are ranked by percentage completion against each participant's target.

☁️ Cloud Sync

Sign in with your Google account in the top bar to save your entries to the live cloud database and have your progress automatically displayed on the group leaderboard.
